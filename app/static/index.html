Why 120 words / 20 overlap?

Each heading section is split into 120-word evidence windows. The 20-word overlap repeats the boundary context so a rule that starts near the end of one window can still be retrieved with the condition that follows in the next window.

The MiniLM comparison covered 60/10, 90/15, 120/20, 160/24, and 220/30. The 120/20, 160/24, and 220/30 settings each produced 182 chunks and tied on the reported retrieval-quality metrics, so 120/20 is the smallest cap in that tied group.

On the expanded corpus, global top-five results covered every expected family in 1 of 5 multi-family queries; MMR at λ=0.5 raised that to 2 of 5 and family recall from 0.76 to 0.81. The production route also recognizes multiple policy families, seeds one result per family, then returns five diverse citations. It covered all expected families in the five labeled multi-family route probes. These small, hand-labeled results are retrieval evidence, not a general answer-quality guarantee.
